Privacy Policy – Clipper

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy describes how Clipper, an internal tool operated by Off The Stream (“we”), handles data. Clipper uses YouTube API Services.

Who uses Clipper

Clipper is a private tool used only by the Off The Stream team to publish clips to our own YouTube channel. It is not available to the public and does not request access to anyone else's account.

What data Clipper accesses

  • Clipper requests a single Google permission: youtube.upload – the ability to upload videos to the authorized YouTube channel.
  • It uploads video files that we created and manually approved, together with the title, description, tags and category we set.
  • It stores the resulting video ID and scheduled publish time locally, to track what was uploaded.
  • Clipper does not read, collect or store viewer data, comments, messages, analytics or any personal information of YouTube users.

How data is stored and shared

  • The Google OAuth access token is stored only on our own computer and is never shared.
  • We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
  • No advertising, tracking cookies or analytics are used by Clipper.

YouTube and Google policies

By using Clipper (YouTube API Services), the authorizing user agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. After revocation, the locally stored token stops working and can be deleted.

Data deletion

Locally stored upload records can be deleted on request. Contact us at the address below.
