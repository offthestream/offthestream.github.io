What we do

Off The Stream publishes short vertical clips (YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels) of memorable moments from public live streams. Each clip is edited – vertical framing and captions – and credits the original creator. All clips belong to their original creators; go follow and support them.

Clipper (our upload tool)

“Clipper” is a private, internal tool used only by the Off The Stream team. It uploads clips that were manually reviewed and approved by us to our own YouTube channel, using the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account. See the Privacy Policy.
